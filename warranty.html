Warranty Terms & Conditions

Important information about our warranty coverage

Warranty Duration

Our warranty is valid for 6 months from the date of repair completion. The warranty period begins when you pick up your device or when it's delivered to you.

Warranty Coverage

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It includes both parts and labor for the specific repair performed. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, abuse, accidents, or normal wear and tear.

Warranty Limitations

This warranty is limited to the repair or replacement of defective parts. We are not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. The warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original customer.

Warranty Service

Warranty service is provided at no charge during normal business hours. We may require proof of purchase and warranty documentation. Service may be performed by us or our authorized service providers.

Warranty Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our warranty coverage

How long is the warranty valid?

Our warranty is valid for 6 months from the date of repair completion. This covers both parts and labor for the specific repair performed.

What if the same problem occurs again?

If the same issue occurs within the warranty period, we'll repair it again at no charge. We'll also investigate to ensure the root cause is properly addressed.

Do I need to keep my receipt?

Yes, please keep your repair receipt as proof of warranty coverage. We may also have your information in our system, but the receipt is the best proof of warranty.

What if I damage the device after repair?

Our warranty only covers defects in our repair work, not new damage caused by misuse, accidents, or other external factors. We'll assess each case individually.

Can I get a refund instead of repair?

In most cases, we prefer to repair the issue under warranty. However, if repair is not possible or practical, we may offer a refund or replacement at our discretion.
