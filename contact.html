Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our repair services

How much does a repair cost?

Repair costs vary depending on the device and issue. We provide free diagnostics and transparent pricing. Most repairs range from $50-$500. Contact us for a free estimate.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are completed within 24-48 hours. Simple repairs like screen replacements can often be done same-day. We'll provide an estimated completion time during diagnostics.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes! We offer free pickup within 10 miles of our Sacramento location. Extended service areas are available with a small pickup fee. Contact us to arrange pickup.

What warranty do you provide?

All repairs come with our comprehensive 6-month warranty covering both parts and labor. We stand behind our work and will fix any issues that arise during the warranty period.

Do you repair all brands?

Yes! We repair all major brands including Apple, Samsung, LG, Sony, Microsoft, and many others. Our technicians are trained on the latest devices and repair techniques.
