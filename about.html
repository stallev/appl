Our Story

Founded in 2014, Sacramento Appliance Repair has been serving the Sacramento community with professional, reliable appliance repair services. What started as a small family business has grown into the most trusted appliance repair service in the area.

Our founder, John Smith, began his career as a certified technician with over 15 years of experience working with major appliance manufacturers. After seeing the need for honest, reliable repair services in Sacramento, he decided to start his own company with a simple mission: to provide exceptional service at fair prices.
