About Sacramento Appliance Pro

Your trusted local appliance repair company serving Sacramento and surrounding areas since 2015

Our Story

Sacramento Appliance Pro was founded in 2015 by Mike Johnson, a Sacramento native with over 15 years of experience in appliance repair. What started as a one-man operation has grown into Sacramento's most trusted appliance repair company, serving thousands of satisfied customers throughout the region.

Our mission is simple: to provide fast, reliable, and affordable appliance repair services that exceed our customers' expectations. We believe that when your appliances break down, you shouldn't have to wait days or weeks for service. That's why we offer same-day service and stand behind our work with comprehensive warranties.

As a locally owned and operated business, we understand the unique needs of Sacramento residents. We're not just your appliance repair company – we're your neighbors, and we're committed to keeping your home running smoothly.
