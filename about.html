Our Story

Sacramento Electronic Repair was founded in 2015 by John Smith, a certified electronics technician with over 15 years of experience in the industry. What started as a small repair shop has grown into Sacramento's most trusted electronic repair service.

Our mission has always been simple: provide fast, reliable, and affordable repair services to our community. We believe that everyone deserves access to quality electronic repair services, and we're committed to making that possible.

Over the years, we've repaired thousands of devices and built lasting relationships with our customers. Our success is measured not just by the number of devices we've fixed, but by the trust and satisfaction of our community.
